SIXORDLE

Unlimited Puzzles

HOW TO PLAY

Solve all 6 hidden words simultaneously in 11 guesses.

Each guess must be a valid 5-letter word. Every guess evaluates across all 6 boards at the same time.

S is in the word and in the correct spot (Green).

A is in the word but in the wrong spot (Yellow).

L, T, E are not in the word (Grey).

Board Locks: Once a board is completely solved (all 5 tiles green), it locks in and stops taking further guesses, allowing you to focus on the remaining open boards.

6-Stripe Smart Keyboard: Each letter key is split into 6 vertical columns corresponding directly to Boards 1 through 6 from left to right. You can immediately see which boards a letter is green, yellow, or eliminated on.

Restart Anytime: Hit the Restart button in the header to spin up an instant fresh game.
